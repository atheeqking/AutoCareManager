# Phase 5 use cases

Customers select one of their vehicles, enter visit details, review the appointment, and confirm it. Workshop staff confirm, cancel, mark no-show, or check in eligible appointments. Check-in creates the initial service case only; diagnosis and repair remain later-phase work.

Workshop staff can create a walk-in by selecting existing Phase 4 customer and vehicle records, entering service type, priority, and the customer's issue, reviewing it, then confirming the creation.

Workshop staff manage the resulting service case: assign an employee, record a diagnosis distinct from the customer issue, obtain the simple workshop approval state, record service items and solution, then mark it ready for pickup. No delivery, payment processing, notification, or service-history workflow is included.
